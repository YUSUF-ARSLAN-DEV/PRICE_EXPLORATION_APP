# Takedown & complaints policy (plan P6) - DRAFT v0.1

**Who can ask:** any retailer, brand, rights holder, or user.
**How:** email [LEGAL EMAIL] (monitored daily) or the web form at [DOMAIN.qa]/report. Include the
page/product/retailer, what you want changed, and your authority to ask.

**Our commitments**
- Acknowledge within 1 business day.
- For a retailer request to stop collecting/displaying its data: **disable that source within 2
  business days** (admin kill-switch; recorded in `takedown_requests`), then discuss.
- For a wrong-price report: correct or hide the price within 2 business days (faster for
  high-traffic items).
- Content alleged unlawful/defamatory: hidden pending review.
- Keep a log of every request and outcome; review monthly with the Privacy Lead/counsel.
- A cease-and-desist letter is forwarded to counsel the same day and the source is disabled
  immediately.

**Retailers** may also "claim their store" to maintain their own prices (roadmap 11.3).
