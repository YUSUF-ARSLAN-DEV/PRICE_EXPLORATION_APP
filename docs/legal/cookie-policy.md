# Cookie Policy - DRAFT v0.1

| Cookie / storage | Purpose | Type | Duration | Consent |
| ---------------- | ------- | ---- | -------- | ------- |
| `session` (httpOnly) | Keep you signed in | Strictly necessary | session / rotating refresh | Not required |
| `consent` | Remember your cookie choices | Strictly necessary | 12 months | Not required |
| `locale` | Remember language | Strictly necessary | 12 months | Not required |
| localStorage `area`, `basket` | Remember area/basket on your device | Functional (device only) | until cleared | Not required (never sent to us) |
| Analytics (self-hosted, cookieless where possible) | Anonymous usage stats | Analytics | up to 13 months | **Opt-in** |

No advertising or cross-site tracking cookies in v1. Scripts for analytics do not load before consent.
Change your choice anytime via "Cookie settings" in the footer.
