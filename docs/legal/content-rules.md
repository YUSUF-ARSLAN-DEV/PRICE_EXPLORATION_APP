# Content rules (plan 0.5) - DRAFT; counsel to confirm

1. **Restricted products.** Alcohol and tobacco/vape products are **not ingested or displayed** in
   v1 (DB view `public_products` filters `restricted = true`). Pork: decision pending counsel
   (default: exclude). Re-evaluate only with written legal advice.
2. **Sponsored content** is labelled and never reorders organic price results.
3. **User submissions** are moderated; reject personal data, defamation, false claims, content
   contrary to public morals or Islamic values; receipts auto-blurred for PII.
4. **Images:** only our own, licensed, or user-submitted (under ToU licence). No scraped retailer
   images or flyer artwork without a licence.
5. **Claims** ("cheapest", "save X%") must be backed by a dated methodology page.
6. **Language:** all user-facing legal and price information available in Arabic and English.
